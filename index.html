HOW TO PLAY
  • Tap and hold a colored dot, then drag to its matching pair (or any dot sharing its color) to draw a connecting line.
  • Connect every dot to finish the wave and hear the full song play.
  • Longer, more winding lines score more than short direct ones — the scenic route pays better.
  • A line can't cross a barrier (dashed) or another line. Some barriers rotate, and can snap a connection they sweep through even after it's finished.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom, and drag empty space to pan around once you're zoomed in.
  • Tap the ⋮ button any time — it's everything in one place: Hint (flashes every unconnected dot white), Erase a Line (Relaxed/Sleep only — tap it, then tap any line you've drawn to remove it and redraw), saving, restarting, and this help.
  • Later waves can hide a dot behind its own sealed wall, reachable only through a swirling portal pair. Drag to one side to lock in, then start a fresh drag from the other side to reach its match.
  • Flight Mode (title screen toggle): pilot a ship instead of dragging — hold anywhere to steer it, and fly through one dot then another to connect them, momentum and all.
  • Cockpit Mode (title screen toggle): a first-person 3D view — hold anywhere as a joystick to bank and thrust, and fly through one dot then another to connect them. The ship drifts on release instead of stopping dead.
